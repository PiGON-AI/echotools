# EchoTools

**Milo, the AI companion for your coding partner, in three parts:**

| | What it does | Get it |
|---|---|---|
| **EchoVoice** | Hear your AI coding partner. Free local neural voices. | [Marketplace](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=PigonAI.echo-voice) · [Open VSX](https://open-vsx.org/extension/PigonAI/echo-voice) |
| **EchoAvatar** | See your AI partner: Milo, a living avatar who listens and dances. Needs EchoVoice. | [Marketplace](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=PigonAI.echoavatar) · [Open VSX](https://open-vsx.org/extension/PigonAI/echoavatar) |
| **EchoMemory** | Give your AI partner a memory your whole crew shares. Needs EchoVoice and EchoAvatar. | Coming soon |

No telemetry. No crypto token. No account. Your memory stays on your machine.
